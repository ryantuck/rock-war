Rules overview

[AI - Claude]: Rules overview

Goal. Destroy the enemy's army on the board, leave it with no legal action, or control three obelisks at once.

Pieces

PieceValuePer army

  • 45 points per army. Any piece worth 2 or more is a warrior.
  • Every piece starts in your sideboard, your off-board reserve.
  • Destroyed and sacrificed pieces leave the game for good.
  • Returned pieces go back to the sideboard and can come out again.

Board & placement

Placement. Armies place one scout at a time on any empty territory, in snake order A B B A A B … B places last, then A takes the first turn.

The layout sets the board, the obelisk spots and the starting scouts:

4×4 partitiondefault
Every territory touches exactly one obelisk. 5 scouts each, and they may double up.
5×5 classic
One obelisk in each corner block. The middle row and column touch none. 5 scouts each.
4×4 diamondexperimental
Obelisks ring the center: center territories touch two, corners none. 3 scouts each.
hex13
13 hexagons in rows of 1-3-5-3-1. Each obelisk touches three territories; the center touches none. 5 scouts each, and they may double up.
  • Adjacent means sharing an edge: up to 4 neighbors on squares (no diagonals), up to 6 on hexes.
  • Doubling up: a placement may go on one of your own lone scouts instead of an empty territory.

Territories & contingents

  • A territory holds one army's pieces, 2 at most.
  • Pair rule: two pieces can share a territory only if their values sit next to each other in 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8.
11 21 32 53 85
  • A contingent is a connected group of your territories. Its strength is the total value of its pieces.

  • Contingents and their strengths are fixed when your turn starts.
  • A territory that a contingent moves into or captures joins it for the rest of the turn.

Your turn

Each of your contingents takes up to 2 actions. Their costs together can't exceed its strength.

Two warriors strike in one action: at two territories, each strong enough alone, or together at one, with combined value at least its total.
  • Turn 1: the opening player acts with only one contingent.
  • Spawn needs room under the pair rule: a territory holding a lone scout or a lone soldier.
  • Move obeys the pair rule. An empty territory counts as 0 when working out the cost.
  • Evolve returns both pieces to your sideboard, and the new piece must be available there.
  • Coordinated attack across two contingents: each pays for its own striker and uses one of its actions.

Combat

  1. Retreat. Each defender may retreat free into an adjacent territory its army already holds. One scout per attack may retreat into an adjacent empty territory instead.
  2. Fight. Defenders that stayed die. The attacker's fate depends on how much defense stayed:
Nothing stayed
The attacker moves in intact.
Half its value or less
The attacker moves in intact.
3attacks1→3moves in
More than half
The attacker moves in and devolves into its two parts: 3 into 2+1, 5 into 3+2, 8 into 5+3.

Equal to its value

3attacks21→ all three pieces die
  • Retreats obey the pair rule.
  • A devolving piece returns to the sideboard and its parts deploy from there. A part that isn't available is lost.
  • After mutual destruction the territory is left empty.

Coordinated attacks

  • Defenders can't retreat into any territory that is also under attack.
  • Each territory fights the total value striking it:
Equal
Every striker on it dies.

Obelisks

Four obelisks stand on points where territories meet. Each one boosts one action.

You control an obelisk when all three hold
  • You occupy at least 2 of the territories it touches.
  • Your total value on them, your fervor, is at least 3.
  • Your fervor is strictly higher than the enemy's.
Bonus budget
Extra budget for the obelisk's action. It can also pay for extra actions past the 2-action cap.
Ability
Sacrifice one of your pieces on a territory it touches as fuel, of value F. The effect reaches anywhere on the board. Once per obelisk per turn, on your turn or the enemy's.
Aura

Ability
Send one enemy piece worth F or less back to its owner's sideboard.
Aura

  • Bonus budget is shared by your whole army and refills every turn. Contingents spend their own strength first. An extra action past the cap must be paid for entirely from the bonus.
  • An ability on your turn uses one of the fueling contingent's 2 actions and no budget. It can't be taken as an extra action.
  • An ability on the enemy's turn is free. After each enemy action you may fire any ability not yet used that turn.
  • Fire damage lowers a territory's total value. What is left re-forms from its owner's sideboard. Parts that are unavailable or don't fit are lost.
  • Earth's new piece must be available in your sideboard, as with a normal evolve.
  • If fervor is level, nobody controls the obelisk.

Winning

Elimination
The enemy has no pieces left on the board. You win.
Three obelisks
You control any 3 at once. This is checked after every action and ability, on either turn, and the move that completes it wins on the spot.
No legal action
The enemy starts a turn unable to act. You win.
Turn limit
After 200 turns (100 each), the army with more total value on the board wins. If level, more obelisks wins. If still level, it's a draw.
Draw
Also when a single fight wipes both armies off the board.

Playing against an engine

  1. Pick human for either engine, then press New game.
  2. Placement: click a territory outlined in dashed green.
  3. On your turn, click one of your territories to select it (gold). Then click green to move there or red to attack. Buttons appear for spawn, evolve and obelisk abilities; ability targets show in dashed blue.
  4. Press end turn when done.

Every tunable knob and the simulation findings are in rockwar/README.md.

Not offered to the human side yet: choosing retreats (they are planned automatically), abilities on the engine's turn, coordinated attacks, and split fire hits.
