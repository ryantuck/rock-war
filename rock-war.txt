ROCK WAR [Fibonacci Variant]

Rock War is a simple game designed to be played using an arbitrary map and an arbitrary number
of rocks, doodads, or trinkets you have lying around.

Armies wage war in an all-out battle royale via territorial expansion, combat, and the elemental
magic of the obelisks.

----------------------------------------------

How to Play

1. Setup 	Select map, armies, and obelisks, and place initial rocks
2. Play		Armies alternate moving, evolving, attacking, and spawning
3. Win		Game ends when one army can do nothing else

MECHANICS / TURNS

    Armies alternate turns moving, evolving, attacking, and spawning.

    MV:     Move                N territories               1 - 20

    SPAWN:  spawn               1 + N obelisks              1 - 6
    OB:     Trigger obelisk     N obelisks                  1 - 5

    Move / Attack:              N territories               1-20
    Spawn / Evolve:             Max warrior strength        2, 3, 5, 8

        MY ARMY         OPPONENT        LOCATION

        Evolve
        Obelisks        Obelisks

                        Defend          Map -> Graveyard

SPAWN   Spawn                           Reserves -> Map


MECHANICS / EVOLUTION

    Pieces evolve into one another according to the Fibonacci sequence

    Pieces must occupy the same territory to evolve

    [1] [1] ==> [2]
    [1] [2] ==> [3]
    [2] [3] ==> [5]
    [3] [5] ==> [8]


MECHANICS / MOVEMENT

    Only scouts may move into open territory








----------------------------------------------

MAP

    A map is an arbitrary graph of territories.

    It can be a grid, a honeycomb like Settlers, or a realistic map like Risk.


MAP / TERRITORIES

    Only one army can occupy any given territory.

    Army Size       Map Size        N Obelisks
            2                5              0
            3                8              1
            5               13              2
            8               21              3
           13               34              5
           21               55              8
           34               89             13


MAP / TERRITORIES / CAPACITY

    Each territory supports 1, 2, 3, 5, or 8 total value.

    All territories must contain valid rock combinations (e.g. 3+5, 3+1+1) after every movement


MAP / TERRITORIES / ELEVATION

    Some territories are elevated

    Elevated territories cost an additional movement to access

    Warriors attacking from above get +1 attack

COMBAT

    One or more warriors attack into a single target territory.

    Rocks in defending territory may retreat into valid friendly territories (eg 2 into 3).

    Defending army assigns defending rocks to attacking rocks one-to-one.

    Blocks are applied one at a time.

    Defending army may decide to counter-attack after initial blocks.

    Combat rules are applied recursively as pieces devolve in combat.
