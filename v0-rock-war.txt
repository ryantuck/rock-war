ROCK WAR

Rock War is a simple game designed to be played using an arbitrary map and an arbitrary number of rocks, doodads, or trinkets you have lying around.

Armies wage war in an all-out battle royale throughout the four seasons via expansion, combat, and the elemental magic of the obelisks.

Spring - The game begins at the Spring equinox, where our budding armies stake out territory and begin to expand turn-by-turn, seeking nourishing energy from the obelisks.

Summer - As armies grow, summer begins, and strategic use of the obelisks will turn nature against your enemy to stymie their territorial goals. 

Fall - As Fall commences at the height of the game, the obelisks power grows, unleashing deadly forces of nature against vast contingents, for a mortal cost.

Winter - As the body count piles up for both armies, Winter begins, and all hope is lost as the obelisks turn hostile and indiscriminately barrage both armies with the greatest catastrophes nature can summon as a winter storm bears down turn-by-turn from the North. Armies offer sacrifices of their power to save their contingents from the onslaught turn-by-turn as they now merely battle for the honor to die last against the epic strength and indifference of Nature to claim yet another battle in the endless Rock War.

----------------------------------------------

How to Play

1. Design - select map, armies, and obelisks
2. Placement - place obelisks, then place two scouts each, snake-style
3. Gameplay - each contingent pays up to N for further ramp, movement, and combat
4. End-game - game ends when one army can do nothing else

1. MAP

A map is an arbitrary graph of territories. It can be a grid, a honeycomb like Settlers, or a realistic map like Risk.

Map / Territories

- Only one army can occupy any given territory
- By default, a territory has a maximum capacity of 7 points of pieces


Map / Territories / Terrain

- Territories can be a certain type of terrain that affects stats and movement of pieces
- Water
- (Settlers variant - sheep, wheat, ore, brick, log)


----------------------------------------------


2. ARMIES

Each Rock War battle is between two or more armies of the same total strength N.

Armies / Pieces

- Scouts - 1-value pieces core to expanding territory and enabling movement
- Warriors - 2+ value pieces with unique abilities
- Transports - Pieces capable of transporting N other non-transport pieces


----------------------------------------------


3. OBELISKS


Obelisks / Basics

- Core abilties engine
- Evolve with seasons
- Each army may trigger a return or sacrifice ability exactly once per obelisk
- Each army can trigger one obelisk per turn (?)


Obelisks / Seasonal Mechanics

Spring      Adjacency       Adjacency is nourishing
Summer      Return X        Abilities are strategic and disruptive
Fall        Sacrifice X     Abilities are strategic and lethal
Winter      Pay X Ramp      Avoid destruction by payment alone


Obelisks / Mechanics / Adjacency

- immediately adjacent vs once removed vs adjacent contingent vs shared territory (???)
- balance of piece counts
- spring only vs always (depleting benefits)
- n territories vs n pieces



----------------------------------------------

4. SEASONS

Seasons are phases of the game triggered by piece counts on the board and graveyard


Seasons / Spring

- Placement occurs at Spring equinox
- Obelisks provide adjacent territories and contingents with benefits


Seasons / Summer

- Summer solstice occurs when 50% of pieces are on the board
- Obelisks provide the ability to return pieces in exchange for strategic maneuvering of enemy forces


Seasons / Fall

- Fall equinox when all of one army is on the board (?)
- Obelisks abilities turn lethal, with greater costs


Seasons / Winter

- Winter solstice triggered when 50% of pieces are in graveyard
- Storm from North
- Obelisks turn hostile, pay ramp costs to avoid destruction and survive long enough to win

----------------------------------------------

5. PLACEMENT

- At the beginning of the game, each army places two scouts anywhere on the board, in snake style
- Scouts cannot initially be placed next to obelisks

----------------------------------------------

6. CONTINGENTS

- A contingent is a connected set of territories occupied by the same army
- A contingent has X to spend each turn on ramp, abilities, movement, and combat, where X is the total value of its pieces
- Each contingent may place up to one piece each turn

----------------------------------------------

7. RAMP

Ramp describes how armies populate onto the board. Each contingent may place up to one piece per turn.


----------------------------------------------

8. MOVEMENT

- Movement costs the value of the piece moving
- Only scouts may move into open territory
- Warriors may only move into territories occupied by scouts of the same army


----------------------------------------------

9. COMBAT


Combat / Attack / Mechanics

- Attacking team pays sum of attacking pieces in territory X
- Entire territory attacks as unit (?)
- Pay +1 for each additional attacking territory
- Defending player decides how damage is dealt



Combat / Attack / Hierarchy

- (3-warrior) beats (2-warrior + 1-scout) beats (3x 1-scouts)



Combat / Attack / Transports

- Transports sustain (initial? all?) damage from an attack before



Combat / Attack / Etc

- Scouts - 3+ scouts combined can attack together
- Light transports allow for retreat
